# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step (stated by the user's plan; deployed on GitHub Pages). Backend: Supabase (free tier). Mail: Google Apps Script.

## Users
Hugo English Club ("Niffler" team), fewer than 20 people, Vietnamese speakers. Admins (club leads) assign work and read overdue alerts; members check their tasks on phone or laptop, move cards, submit proof files, comment. (Inferred from the brief: roles and sizes confirmed by the user in conversation.)

## Product Purpose
Track each member's work progress without anyone's laptop staying on. Admin drops one CSV to distribute tasks to everyone; members move cards Nhận -> Đang làm -> Hoàn thành and must upload proof to finish; every change is logged; meetings logs and an overall timeline live in the same place; email reminders go out before deadlines and admins are told when work is late. Success: nobody has to ask "ai đang làm gì, trễ chưa?" in chat.

## Positioning
A club-sized tracker with the club's identity, not a generic project tool: one-file bulk assignment, proof-gated completion, built-in guides and templates in Vietnamese. (Inferred.)

## Brand
Logo: Hugo English Club "NIFFLER" (round purple ring, Niffler creature with yellow-black scarf, magenta "NIFFLER" lettering). Kept at `assets/logo.png`. Voice: friendly, short Vietnamese; controls name their action.

## Constraints
Free tier only, no credit card. Mail sent from the club Gmail via Apps Script (no password stored anywhere). Accessibility: keyboard alternative for every drag-drop action. Phone-width usable.

## Open decisions
Visual world (decided later in new-work, derived from the logo palette). Surface mode: Operate.
