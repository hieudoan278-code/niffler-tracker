# Member guide

## Signing in
Click **Sign in with Google** using the exact email an admin added for you. If it says "Not on the list", ask an admin to add that email.

On your first sign-in you are asked for **your name**: enter your real name so everyone recognises you. To change it later, click **Rename** in the top-right corner. Use the **English / Tiếng Việt** button next to it to switch the interface language.

## Seeing your tasks
On the **Task board** tab, the first row is yours. Each task is a card in one of 3 columns:
- **To do**: newly assigned.
- **Doing**: you have started.
- **Done**: finished, with proof submitted.

Tick **My tasks only** to hide other people's rows. A red "late" label means the deadline has passed; a yellow label means less than 24 hours are left. If tasks belong to an event, the card shows the event name and you can filter by event.

## Moving a card
- **Computer:** drag the card to another column in your row.
- **Phone or keyboard:** click the card, then use the "Move to …" buttons under it.

## Completing a task
Moving a card to **Done** requires a **proof file** (screenshot, PDF, your work file…). Drop the file in the box or click to choose, then click **Submit and complete**. If you cancel, the card stays where it was.

> No proof, no Done. This is enforced by the system and cannot be bypassed.

## Details, comments, history
Click a card to open the side panel: description, the file the admin attached, your proof file, **Comments** (discuss right on the card) and **Activity history** (who changed what, and when).

## Timeline and meeting log
- **Timeline**: a long bar for each task from the day it was assigned to its deadline, a pink line for today, yellow diamonds for meetings and events.
- **Meeting log**: read the notes of past meetings, with a search box.

## Reminder emails
You get an email from `niffler.hugo@gmail.com` when a task has less than 24 hours left. If you can't find it, check your Spam folder.
